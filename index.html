The Story

Stone doesn’t melt.
Until it does.

In the old myth, Medusa turned anyone who met her gaze into stone. Hard. Silent. Unmoving.

We took that idea and turned it around. Stone Melt is the moment the hardness softens. The rush slows down, and the day finally lets go.

That is the mark we want to leave on you.
