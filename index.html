Stone doesn't melt. Until it does.

In the old story, one look from Medusa turned everything to stone. STON MELT is about the opposite moment: the pause when something hard softens.

Real brewed coffee, made by hand, in drinks you have not seen before. Crafted to leave a mark.

The space

A clean, aesthetic store inside Central Mall, Visakhapatnam. Metal, black and a little red, built around the coffee.
